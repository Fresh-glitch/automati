## Automati 1.11.1 - The Housekeeping Update

A look under the hood. Your machines work exactly as before, but the code that runs them is leaner, which makes the next machines quicker to build.

### Changed
- **Electric Furnace mode button**: now a proper pixel-art furnace face in the top-left corner of the GUI. Click it to switch between Furnace and Blast Furnace processing.
- **Electric Furnace experience**: shift-clicking the output now pays out the stored smelting experience, just like a normal click.

### Under the hood
- Machines, conduits and GUIs share far more code: about a quarter less of it overall.
- Generators, cables and Erg Jars do less work every tick when handing out energy.

Existing worlds load as before.
